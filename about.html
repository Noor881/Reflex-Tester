About ReflexTester.fun

Helping gamers and athletes unlock their peak performance through precision reflex training.

Our Mission

ReflexTester.fun was created with a simple goal: provide free, professional-grade reflex testing tools to everyone. Whether you're a competitive esports player looking to shave milliseconds off your reaction time or someone curious about their cognitive abilities, our platform offers the tools you need.

Why Reflexes Matter

In competitive gaming, the difference between victory and defeat often comes down to fractions of a second. A 50ms improvement in reaction time can mean the difference between landing a headshot or getting eliminated first. But reflex training isn't just for gamers—athletes, drivers, surgeons, and countless other professionals rely on quick reactions in their daily work.
