Our Story

ReflexTester.fun started in 2023 as a side project. We were frustrated that the best reaction time testing tools were either locked behind paywalls, riddled with ads, or scientifically inaccurate. Most browser-based testers had input lag of 20-50ms baked in and never mentioned it.

We spent months researching reaction time science — reading studies from the Journal of Motor Behavior, sports psychology research, and esports performance labs — before writing a single line of code. The result is a platform where every tool is built around one principle: accurate measurement first, everything else second.

Today, ReflexTester.fun hosts 31+ free tools used by competitive gamers, athletes, coaches, physical therapists, researchers, and anyone curious about their own cognitive performance. We have never charged a single cent and we never will for core features.

Why Reaction Time Matters

Reaction time is the time elapsed between a stimulus and your voluntary response to it. It sounds simple, but it sits at the intersection of neuroscience, motor learning, and athletic performance. Your reaction time is a real, measurable indicator of your nervous system's efficiency.

In competitive gaming, a 50ms improvement in reaction time is the difference between landing a shot and getting eliminated. In driving, a 100ms improvement is roughly 3 metres of stopping distance at highway speeds. In medical settings, reaction time decline is an early biomarker for conditions like Parkinson's, concussion, and cognitive aging.

The science is clear: reflex speed is trainable. Regular, deliberate practice using the right protocols can improve reaction time by 40-80ms in most healthy adults, regardless of age or starting point. The key is measuring accurately so you can train intelligently and track real progress — not guesswork.

How Our Tools Work

Every test on ReflexTester.fun uses performance.now() — a high-resolution timer accurate to fractions of a millisecond in modern browsers — instead of the imprecise Date.now() used by most websites. This eliminates the 15-60ms measurement error common on other platforms.

On-Device Privacy

All your test results are stored locally in your browser's localStorage. Nothing is sent to our servers. Your scores, history, and personal bests live on your own device and are never shared with us or third parties. You can clear them anytime from the Dashboard page.
