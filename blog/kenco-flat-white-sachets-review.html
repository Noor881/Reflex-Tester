What to consider before buying

This is not the same experience as an espresso-based flat white from a café. The recipe is pre-mixed, so you cannot independently adjust the coffee, milk and sweetness. Check the current ingredient and allergen panel if you avoid dairy, sugar or particular additives. A 40-sachet pack also makes most sense if you already know you enjoy this style.

Caffeine and performance: caffeine can improve alertness for many adults, but sensitivity varies. Avoid relying on coffee to replace sleep, consider your total daily intake, and avoid late use if it disrupts rest. Pregnant people and anyone with a medical concern should follow professional guidance.

A simple desk-break routine

  1. Step away from the reaction-time test or game for five minutes.
  2. Prepare the sachet using the current pack directions.
  3. Drink some water as well, especially during long sessions.
  4. Return only when you feel refreshed rather than using caffeine to push through fatigue.
