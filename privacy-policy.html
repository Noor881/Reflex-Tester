ReflexTester.fun Privacy Policy

Your Privacy Matters: We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we collect, use, and protect your information. This policy explains our practices in plain language.

1. Information We Collect

Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage Data: Reaction times, test scores, number of tests completed
  • Technical Data: Browser type, device information, IP address, screen resolution
  • Analytics Data: Page views, time spent on site, referring websites (via Google Analytics)
  • Performance Data: Test completion rates, error rates, session duration

Information You Provide (Optional)

  • Contact Information: Email address if you contact us
  • User Profile: Age, gaming experience (if you choose to provide)
  • Feedback: Comments, suggestions, or bug reports you submit

2. How We Use Your Information

Primary Uses

  • Service Provision: Display your test results and statistics
  • Performance Tracking: Calculate averages, best times, and improvement metrics
  • Site Improvement: Analyze usage patterns to enhance user experience
  • Technical Support: Troubleshoot issues and provide customer support

Analytics and Research

  • Aggregate Analysis: Create anonymized statistics about reaction times by age, device, etc.
  • Performance Benchmarks: Develop average reaction time standards
  • Scientific Research: Contribute to reaction time studies (anonymized data only)

3. Data Storage and Security

Local Storage

Your test results are primarily stored locally in your browser using:

  • Browser Storage: Test scores, statistics, and preferences
  • Session Data: Temporary data during active testing sessions
  • Cache: Website files for faster loading

Security Measures

  • HTTPS Encryption: All data transmission is encrypted
  • Secure Hosting: Website hosted on secure, monitored servers
  • Access Controls: Limited access to any collected data
  • Regular Updates: Security patches and updates applied promptly

4. Cookies and Tracking

Essential Cookies

  • Functionality: Remember your test preferences and settings
  • Performance: Store your test results and statistics
  • Security: Prevent fraudulent activity

Analytics Cookies (Google Analytics)

  • Usage Analytics: Understand how visitors use our site
  • Performance Metrics: Track site speed and functionality
  • User Behavior: Analyze navigation patterns (anonymized)
Cookie Control: You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but this may affect site functionality and your ability to track personal progress.

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We DO NOT Share Personal Data

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Limited Sharing Scenarios

  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect our rights
  • Service Providers: Google Analytics (anonymized data only)
  • Aggregated Data: Anonymous statistics may be shared for research purposes

6. Your Rights and Choices

Data Control

  • Clear Data: Reset your statistics and clear local storage anytime
  • Opt-Out Analytics: Use browser settings to opt out of Google Analytics
  • Contact Us: Request information about data we may have collected
  • Data Deletion: Request deletion of any contact information we may have

Browser Controls

  • Cookie Management: Block or delete cookies through browser settings
  • Do Not Track: We respect browser Do Not Track signals
  • Private Browsing: Use incognito/private mode for no data storage

7. International Users

ReflexTester.fun is accessible worldwide. If you are located outside the United States:

  • Data Transfer: Your data may be processed in the United States
  • GDPR Compliance: EU users have additional rights under GDPR
  • Local Laws: We comply with applicable privacy laws in your jurisdiction

8. Children's Privacy

Our service is designed for users of all ages, including children:

  • No Personal Data Required: Children can use the site without providing personal information
  • Parental Guidance: We recommend parental supervision for users under 13
  • Educational Use: Tests are suitable for educational and developmental purposes
  • Contact for Concerns: Parents can contact us regarding any privacy concerns

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons:

  • Notification: Significant changes will be prominently displayed on our website
  • Effective Date: Changes become effective immediately upon posting
  • Review Regularly: We recommend reviewing this policy periodically

  • User Control: You control your data and can delete it anytime
  • Transparency: This policy explains our practices in clear, understandable language
