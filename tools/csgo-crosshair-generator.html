Crosshair Style Guide

  • Style 0/1 (Default): Modern style, expands when moving
  • Style 2/3 (Classic): CS 1.6 style, splits when firing
  • Style 4 (Static): Never moves — most popular for competitive

Crosshair Tips

  • Color: Contrast with map backgrounds (cyan/green work well on most maps)
  • Size & Gap: Negative gap overlaps lines, larger gaps for spray control
  • Outline: Essential for visibility on bright surfaces like dust2
  • Dot: Helps with precise one-taps, can obscure enemies at distance
