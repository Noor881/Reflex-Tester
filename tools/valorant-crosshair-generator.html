Crosshair Settings

About Valorant Crosshairs

Your crosshair is one of the most important settings in Valorant. The right crosshair improves accuracy, consistency, and confidence in gunfights. Use this generator to experiment with different styles and find what works best for you.

How to Import Your Crosshair

  1. Copy the code from the generator above
  2. Open Valorant and go to Settings → Crosshair
  3. Click "Import Profile Code" at the bottom
  4. Paste the code and press Enter

Pro Player Crosshairs

  • TenZ: Small cyan crosshair with no center dot — maximizes precision
  • Shroud: Classic white cross with small center dot — balanced and clear
  • Hiko: Green cross with thick lines — high visibility
  • Wardell: Thin white crosshair — minimal distraction for AWP shots

Crosshair Tips

  • Color: Use cyan or green for visibility on most maps
  • Size: Smaller crosshairs improve precision, larger ones help with spray control
  • Center Dot: Adds precision but can obscure targets at range
  • Outline: Makes your crosshair visible on any background
