About the Number Speed Test

The number speed test measures how quickly you can recognize and respond to numerical stimuli. This involves symbol recognition, short-term memory recall, and motor response — all compressed into milliseconds.

Why Number Processing Matters

  • In-game HUD reading: Process ammo counts, health values, and timers instantly
  • Economy management: Quick calculations for buy rounds and resource tracking
  • Callouts: Instantly recognize and communicate enemy counts and positions

Tips for Improvement

  • Use the number row keys rather than the numpad for faster access
  • Keep your fingers positioned near the number keys during practice
  • Focus on the center of the screen where the number appears
