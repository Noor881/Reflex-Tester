About the Typing Speed Test

Typing speed is measured in WPM (words per minute), where a "word" is standardized as 5 characters including spaces. Professional typists average 60-80 WPM, while competitive typists can exceed 150 WPM.

WPM Benchmarks

  • 20-40 WPM: Beginner — Hunt and peck typing
  • 40-60 WPM: Average — Typical office worker
  • 60-80 WPM: Fast — Above average, good for productivity
  • 80-100 WPM: Very Fast — Professional level
  • 100+ WPM: Expert — Competitive typist level

Tips to Improve

  • Learn proper touch typing technique — keep fingers on home row
  • Practice daily for 15-20 minutes for consistent improvement
  • Focus on accuracy first, then speed will follow
  • Use all ten fingers and avoid looking at the keyboard
