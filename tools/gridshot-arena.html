About Gridshot Arena

Gridshot is the most popular aim training drill in the world. Targets appear on screen and you click them as fast as possible. Our version adds combo multipliers and particle effects for an engaging game experience.

Scoring System

  • Base hit: 100 points per target
  • Combo bonus: x2 at 5 streak, x3 at 10, x5 at 20+
  • Speed bonus: Faster clicks = more points
  • Misses: Reset your combo streak

Tips for High Scores

  • Prioritize accuracy over speed — combos are worth more than raw clicks
  • Keep your crosshair centered between targets
  • Use wrist movement for small targets, arm for large flicks
  • Practice daily for 5-10 minutes for consistent improvement

What This Trains

Aiming consistency, first-shot placement, target switching, and the rhythm you need before queueing into a match.

How To Use It Well

Run one short round, adjust only one setting, then repeat. Keep your wrist, sensitivity, and display refresh rate consistent while comparing scores.

Best For

  • Warm-ups before ranked games
  • Mouse control practice
  • Finding weak spots in aim mechanics
