About the Click Speed Test

The CPS (clicks per second) test measures how rapidly you can click your mouse button. This skill is particularly important in games like Minecraft PvP, where higher click rates can give you a significant advantage in combat.

Click Techniques

  • Regular clicking: 4-6 CPS — Standard clicking with your index finger
  • Jitter clicking: 8-14 CPS — Tensing your arm to create rapid vibrations
  • Butterfly clicking: 15-25+ CPS — Alternating between two fingers rapidly
  • Drag clicking: 25-100+ CPS — Dragging finger across textured mouse buttons

Average CPS Benchmarks

  • 1-4 CPS: Slow — Below average clicking speed
  • 5-7 CPS: Average — Normal clicking for most users
  • 8-10 CPS: Fast — Above average, good for gaming
  • 11-14 CPS: Very Fast — Competitive level for Minecraft PvP
  • 15+ CPS: Exceptional — Pro-level clicking techniques required
