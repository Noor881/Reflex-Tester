About COD Aim Training

Call of Duty requires fast target acquisition and precise snap aiming. This military-style shooting range uses silhouette targets that simulate real engagement scenarios from Warzone and multiplayer matches.

Training Modes

  • Quick Scope: Targets pop up and disappear - snap to them before they drop back down
  • Spray Control: Strafing targets - practice maintaining aim while targets move sideways
  • Headshot Only: Smaller head-zone targets - develop precision for one-shot eliminations
