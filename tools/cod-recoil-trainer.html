About COD Recoil Control

Recoil control separates average players from pros. Mastering spray patterns allows you to beam enemies at range and maintain accuracy during sustained fire.

Weapon Types

  • Assault Rifles: Moderate vertical recoil, slight horizontal kick
  • SMGs: Lower recoil, faster fire rate, close-range focus
  • LMGs: Heavy vertical recoil, requires strong compensation

Recoil Control Tips

  • Pull Down: Gradually pull your mouse/stick down while firing
  • Micro-Adjustments: Make small left/right corrections for horizontal recoil
  • Burst Fire: Tap-fire at long range to reset recoil
  • Attachments: Use foregrips and compensators to reduce recoil
