About CS:GO Utility

Mastering grenades is crucial in CS:GO. Proper utility usage can win rounds through site execution, retakes, and area control.

Essential Grenades

  • Smoke: Block vision for safe site entry or post-plant defense
  • Flashbang: Blind enemies before peeking or rushing
  • Molotov/Incendiary: Clear corners, delay pushes, post-plant damage
  • HE Grenade: Tag enemies, finish low HP targets

Practice Tips

  • Jump Throws: Use jump-throw binds for consistent smoke lineups
  • Map Knowledge: Learn 5-10 essential smokes per map
  • Team Play: Coordinate utility with teammates for site executes
  • Economy: Know when to save utility and when to use it

What This Trains

Setup confidence: crosshair tuning, recoil familiarity, sensitivity matching, lineups, and loadout planning.

How To Use It Well
