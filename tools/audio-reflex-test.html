About the Audio Reflex Test

The audio reflex test measures your reaction time to sound. Unlike visual stimuli, auditory signals are processed faster by the brain — the auditory cortex responds more quickly to sudden sounds than the visual cortex does to color changes. This is why most people score 20-40ms faster on audio tests.

Why Audio Reflexes Matter

In gaming, audio cues are just as important as visual information:

  • Footsteps: Hear enemies before you see them in CS:GO and Valorant
  • Gunfire direction: React to shots faster with good audio awareness
  • Ability sounds: Respond to ultimates and special abilities instantly
  • Environmental cues: Doors, reloads, and item pickups all provide audio information

Tips for Better Audio Reaction

  • Use quality headphones — they reduce latency compared to speakers
  • Minimize background noise for clearer audio perception
  • Focus on anticipating the sound, not predicting it
