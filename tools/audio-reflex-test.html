About the Audio Reflex Test

The audio reflex test measures your reaction time to sound only — the screen gives no visual cue when the beep fires. This isolates your auditory processing pathway and is typically 20–40ms faster than visual reaction time.

Why Audio Reflexes Matter in Gaming

  • Footsteps in CS2 and Valorant: Hear enemies before you see them
  • Gunfire direction: React to shots faster with audio awareness
  • Ability sounds: Respond to ultimates and special abilities instantly
  • Door creaks, reloads, item pickups: All provide critical audio information

Tips for Better Audio Reaction

  • Use quality headphones — they reduce latency vs. speakers
  • Minimize background noise for clearer audio perception
  • Focus your attention — don't anticipate or predict the beep
