About Valorant Aim Training

Valorant rewards precision and tactical positioning above all else. This 3D tactical range helps you develop the crisp headshots and crosshair discipline needed to climb the ranks. Practice with game-accurate mechanics to build muscle memory that translates directly to competitive matches.

Training Modes

  • Headshot Flicks: Instant headshot practice at varying distances — critical for agent abilities like Jett dashes and Chamber ults
  • Tracking: Follow moving targets with precision — essential for spray control with Phantom and Vandal
  • Precision: Smaller head-only targets — master the pixel-perfect aim that separates ranks

Pro Valorant Aim Benchmarks

  • Iron-Silver: 35-45% accuracy, 500+ ms headshot time
  • Gold-Platinum: 50-60% accuracy, 350-450ms headshot time
  • Diamond+: 65%+ accuracy, sub-300ms flick speed
  • Radiant: 75%+ accuracy, sub-200ms precision headshots

Valorant-Specific Tips

  • Crosshair Placement: Always aim head-level at common angles
  • Counter-Strafing: Stop movement before shooting for perfect accuracy
  • One-Taps: Valorant rewards controlled single shots over spraying
