How to Use the Sensitivity Calculator

This calculator helps you maintain consistent aim across different FPS games by converting your mouse sensitivity settings.

Conversion Method

The calculator uses each game's cm/360° (centimeters per 360-degree turn) to ensure your physical mouse movement remains consistent. This means your muscle memory stays intact when switching between games.

Understanding the Numbers

  • Sensitivity: The in-game sensitivity value you should use in the target game
  • cm/360°: How many centimeters you need to move your mouse to turn 360 degrees
  • eDPI: Effective DPI (your mouse DPI × in-game sensitivity) — higher = faster aim

Pro Gamer eDPI Reference

  • Valorant: 200-400 eDPI (most pros use 280-320)
  • CS:GO: 600-1000 eDPI (most pros use 800-880)
  • Apex Legends: 800-1200 eDPI (movement-heavy, higher sens)
  • COD Warzone: 3200-5600 eDPI (fast-paced gameplay)

Tips for Finding Your Perfect Sens

  • Lower sensitivity = More precision, better for tactical shooters (Valorant, CS:GO)
  • Higher sensitivity = Faster turns, better for battle royales (Apex, Fortnite)
  • Most pros use 25-35 cm/360° for tactical games, 15-25 cm/360° for fast-paced games
  • Test your converted sensitivity in aim trainers before jumping into ranked!
