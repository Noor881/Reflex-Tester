About Spidershot

Spidershot tests both speed and precision. Unlike static targets, these shrink over time, forcing you to react quickly. As your combo grows, targets spawn faster and shrink more aggressively.

Why Shrinking Targets?

  • Time pressure: Simulates moving enemies that disappear behind cover
  • Progressive difficulty: Gets harder as you improve within a round
  • Precision under stress: Small targets demand accuracy even when rushing
