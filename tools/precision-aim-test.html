What Does Precision Aim Measure?

Precision aim tests your ability to place your cursor exactly on small targets — the same skill used in FPS headshots, MOBA skill shots, and RTS micro-management. This test tracks both hit/miss ratio AND average miss distance in pixels.

Precision Benchmarks

  • 90%+ Accuracy: Elite — Pro FPS level precision
  • 75–89%: Great — Strong competitive aim
  • 60–74%: Average — Typical casual gamer
  • Below 60%: Needs training — Practice daily!
