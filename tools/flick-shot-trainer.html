About Flick Shot Training

Flick shots are the foundation of competitive FPS aim. This trainer uses 360-degree random target spawning to build the neural pathways and muscle memory needed for instant target acquisition in any direction.

Training Modes

  • Random 360°: Targets spawn anywhere on screen — trains complete situational awareness and snap aim
  • Wide Flicks: Large angle changes (90°+) — practice extreme flicks for targets behind you
  • Micro Adjustments: Small precise corrections — perfect final crosshair placement

Flick Speed Benchmarks

  • Beginner: 300-400ms average flick time
  • Intermediate: 200-300ms with 60%+ accuracy
  • Advanced: 150-200ms with 70%+ accuracy
  • Pro Level: Sub-150ms flicks with 80%+ accuracy

What Makes Great Flicks

  • Speed + Accuracy: Pros hit both — fast but inaccurate flicks are worthless
  • Muscle Memory: Your hand should "know" common angles without thinking
  • Crosshair Placement: Good pre-aim reduces necessary flick distance
  • Consistency: Hitting 80% of flicks beats hitting 90% once and 50% the next round

When to Use This Trainer

  • Warming up before ranked matches (5-10 minutes)
  • Building speed for AWP/OP/Kraber shots
  • Training 180° reactions for when enemies flank
  • Complementing tracking practice (flicks + tracking = complete aim)
