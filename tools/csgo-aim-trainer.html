About CS:GO Aim Training

Counter-Strike demands precise aim and fast target acquisition. This 3D shooting range simulates realistic scenarios where humanoid targets appear at varying distances and positions, building the muscle memory needed for competitive play.

Training Modes

  • Flick Shot: Targets appear at random positions — practice quick snap aiming like AWP flicks
  • Tracking: Targets strafe side to side — practice smooth crosshair tracking for rifle sprays
  • Precision: Smaller headshot-only targets — develop fine crosshair placement

Pro CS:GO Aim Benchmarks

  • Average player: 40-50% accuracy with 400ms target acquisition
  • Above average: 55-65% accuracy with 300ms target acquisition
  • Pro level: 70%+ accuracy with sub-200ms flick speed
