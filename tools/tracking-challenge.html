About Tracking

Tracking is the ability to keep your crosshair on a moving target. It's critical for weapons with sustained fire like the Vandal, AK-47, R-301, and LMGs. This trainer measures how well you can follow unpredictable movement.

Tracking vs Flicking

  • Tracking: Smooth, continuous aim on moving targets — spraying and beam weapons
  • Flicking: Quick snaps to stationary targets — AWP, headshots, abilities
  • Both are essential: Great aimers combine smooth tracking with sharp flicks

How Scoring Works

  • On target: You're "on target" when your cursor is within the target circle
  • Accuracy %: Percentage of frames where you were on target
  • Smoothness: Measures how steady your cursor is (lower = jittery)

What This Trains

Aiming consistency, first-shot placement, target switching, and the rhythm you need before queueing into a match.

How To Use It Well

Run one short round, adjust only one setting, then repeat. Keep your wrist, sensitivity, and display refresh rate consistent while comparing scores.

Best For

  • Warm-ups before ranked games
  • Mouse control practice
  • Finding weak spots in aim mechanics
