About the Visual Reflex Test

The visual reflex test measures how quickly you can respond to a visual stimulus — the gold standard for gaming performance. When the circle turns green, your visual cortex fires a signal down to your hand in 150–300ms.

Performance Grades

  • Under 150ms — Exceptional: Pro esports level. Fighter pilot territory.
  • 150–200ms — Excellent: Top 5% of gamers. Ready for competitive play.
  • 200–250ms — Good: Above average. Regular training will get you to Excellent.
  • 250–300ms — Average: Normal human range. Room to improve.
  • Over 300ms — Poor: May indicate fatigue, distraction, or need for more practice.

Why This Matters for Gaming

In CS2, Valorant, and Apex Legends, a 50ms difference in reaction time determines who wins gunfights. Train daily for 10–15 minutes to see measurable improvement.
