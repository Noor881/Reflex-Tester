About the Visual Reflex Test

The visual reflex test measures how quickly you can respond to a visual stimulus. When the circle changes from red to green, your brain processes the color change through the visual cortex, then sends a motor command to your hand to click. This entire process typically takes between 150-300 milliseconds for most people.

Why Visual Reaction Time Matters for Gaming

In competitive FPS games like CS:GO, Valorant, and Fortnite, the difference between winning and losing a gunfight often comes down to 50-100ms. Players with faster visual reflexes can:

  • Spot enemies faster and begin aiming sooner
  • React to peek situations more effectively
  • Dodge incoming abilities and projectiles
  • Win more 50/50 engagements against equally-skilled opponents

How to Improve Your Score

  • Practice daily: 10-15 minutes of consistent training yields better results than occasional long sessions
  • Optimize your setup: Use a 144Hz+ monitor and a gaming mouse with low latency
  • Stay alert: Focus your eyes on the center of the circle and be ready to click
  • Get enough sleep: Fatigue can slow reaction times by 20-30%
