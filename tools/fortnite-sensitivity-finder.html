Finding Your Perfect Sensitivity

Fortnite requires balancing two different sensitivities: aim sens for shooting and building sens for fast edits and builds.

Sensitivity Guidelines

  • Low Sens (5-7%): Best for precise aim, harder for building
  • Medium Sens (7-10%): Balanced approach, most popular
  • High Sens (10-15%): Fast builds/edits, requires precise aim

Finding Your Sens

  1. Start Medium: Begin with 8% and 1.8x building multiplier
  2. Test Aim: Can you track moving targets consistently?
  3. Test Building: Can you do 90s and tunnels comfortably?
  4. Adjust: Lower for better aim, higher for faster building
  5. Practice: Give new sens 2-3 days before changing

Pro Player Tips

  • eDPI Range: Most pros use 40-80 eDPI (DPI × Sens%)
  • Building Multiplier: 1.8x - 2.2x is most common
  • Arm vs Wrist: Lower sens = arm aim, higher = wrist aim

Setup confidence: crosshair tuning, recoil familiarity, sensitivity matching, lineups, and loadout planning.

How To Use It Well

Make one change, test it immediately, and save the setting only after it feels stable across multiple attempts.
