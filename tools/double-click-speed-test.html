What Is Double Click Speed?

Double-click speed (DPS — Double-clicks Per Second) measures how quickly you can perform consecutive double-click actions. This matters for drag-select speed in games, file management, and any UI that requires rapid double-inputs.

DPS Benchmarks

  • 1–4 DPS: Average — most casual users
  • 5–8 DPS: Good — experienced PC users
  • 9–12 DPS: Fast — competitive gamers
  • 13+ DPS: Elite — top 1% of clickers

Tips to Improve

  • Use a gaming mouse with a fast debounce time (1ms–4ms)
  • Rest your arm — wrist tension reduces click speed
  • Butterfly clicking (two fingers alternating on one button) can exceed 20 DPS
