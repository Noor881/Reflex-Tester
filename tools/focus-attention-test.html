About the Vigilance / Focus Test

The continuous performance test (CPT) is one of the most widely-used paradigms in cognitive neuroscience. It measures your ability to sustain attention over time — hitting targets while ignoring irrelevant stimuli — and is used in ADHD research, fatigue studies, and cognitive neuroscience.

What the Scores Mean

  • Hits: Correct responses to target — measures detection
  • Misses: Failing to respond to target — attention lapse
  • False Alarms: Responding to non-target — impulse control
  • d' (d-prime): Signal detection sensitivity (high = great focus)

Improving Your Focus

  • 7-9 hours of quality sleep dramatically improves vigilance performance
  • Meditation and mindfulness practice reduces false alarm rate
  • Caffeine (100-200mg) can boost hit rate and reduce reaction time by up to 15%
