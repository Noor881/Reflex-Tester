About Hand-Eye Coordination

Hand-eye coordination is the ability to track targets with your eyes and move your hand to interact with them accurately. 20 targets per session, with difficulty increasing on higher settings. Clicking the background when a target is visible counts as a miss — stay precise!

Why This Matters for Gaming

  • Flick shots: Rapidly moving your aim to new targets
  • Tracking: Following moving enemies smoothly
  • Target switching: Engaging multiple enemies in sequence
  • Micro-adjustments: Fine-tuning aim for headshots

What This Trains

Stimulus recognition, click timing, decision speed, and recovery after missed or early reactions.

How To Use It Well

Take several attempts instead of trusting one result. Reaction time varies with sleep, caffeine, hardware latency, and focus.

Best For

  • Baseline reaction checks
  • Daily speed tracking
  • Comparing visual, audio, and click response
