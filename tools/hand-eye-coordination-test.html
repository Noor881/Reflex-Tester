About Hand-Eye Coordination

Hand-eye coordination is the ability to track targets with your eyes and move your hand to interact with them accurately. This skill is fundamental to FPS gaming, where you need to move your crosshair to enemies as quickly and precisely as possible.

Why This Matters for Gaming

  • Flick shots: Rapidly moving your aim to new targets
  • Tracking: Following moving enemies smoothly
  • Target switching: Engaging multiple enemies in sequence
  • Micro-adjustments: Fine-tuning aim for headshots
