About COD Loadouts

In Call of Duty Warzone and Modern Warfare 3, your loadout is your lifeline. The right combination of weapons, attachments, perks, and equipment can mean the difference between victory and the Gulag. Use this builder to experiment and optimize your setup.

Meta Loadouts (Current Season)

Warzone Meta:

  • RAM-7 + HRM-9: Current AR/SMG meta for Urzikstan
  • SVA 545 + Superi 46: Laser beam accuracy at range
  • Holger 556 + Striker: LMG dominance for squads

MW3 Multiplayer Meta:

  • SVA 545: Zero recoil laser beam
  • Striker SMG: Fastest TTK in close range
  • AMR9: Run and gun king

Loadout Building Tips

  • Warzone: Balance range (AR) with close-quarters power (SMG)
  • Attachments: Prioritize recoil control and mobility
  • Perks: EOD Padding + Ghost are essential for Warzone
  • Field Upgrade: Dead Silence or Trophy System for endgame

How to Use Your Loadout

  1. Build your loadout using the tool above
  2. Copy your share code or screenshot your setup
  3. In Warzone, use Buy Stations to purchase your loadout drop
  4. In MW3, save your custom loadout in the Armory
