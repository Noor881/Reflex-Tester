About the Color Match Test

The color match test measures your visual processing speed and color discrimination ability. When the target color appears, your brain must identify it, compare it against the options, and execute the correct motor response — all as quickly as possible.

Why Color Recognition Matters

  • Enemy identification: Quickly distinguish friend from foe by team colors
  • UI awareness: Process health bars, ammo counts, and ability indicators
  • Environmental cues: Notice colored markers, zones, and hazards
