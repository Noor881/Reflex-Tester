About Fortnite Aim Training

Fortnite demands both building and aiming skills. This 3D arena gives you character-shaped targets at various distances, perfect for warming up before matches or training specific weaknesses in your aim.

Training Modes

  • Shotgun: Close-range targets spawn fast — practice pump flick shots
  • AR Tracking: Moving targets at mid-range — practice smooth tracking aim
  • Edit Play: Small targets appear briefly — simulate quick edit-shot combos
