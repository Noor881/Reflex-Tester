About Apex Recoil Patterns

Unlike other BRs, Apex has predictable recoil patterns. Master them and you'll beam enemies at any range.

Meta Weapons

  • R-301: Smooth vertical pattern, easy to control, versatile
  • R-99: Slight S-curve, extremely high DPS, harder control
  • Flatline: Heavy horizontal kick, high damage, requires practice
  • Volt: Minimal recoil, energy weapon, consistent pattern

Pro Tips

  • Learn Patterns: Apex recoil is 100% consistent - practice offline
  • Barrel Mods: Purple stabilizer reduces recoil by ~40%
  • Jitter Aim: Small rapid movements help track through recoil
  • Close Range: Hip-fire SMGs, ADS for rifles at mid-range
